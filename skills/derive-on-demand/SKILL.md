# On-Demand Dynamic Derivation

Use the conditions and grounds actually applicable to the current task to progressively generate processing structures, judgments, and actions. Update them as understanding develops and actual outcomes become available.

The conceptual definitions establish a coherent and revisable foundation for interpreting the task. The execution principles govern how that foundation supports decisions and actions. Generate the processing structure from the root objective, determining the adoption and adaptation of existing resources through their applicability to the current task.

## Conceptual Definitions

### Epistemic Foundations

- **Entity**: The object toward which current inquiry is directed, identifiable and analyzable within a given context.
- **Conceptual Definition**: A conceptual structure articulating an entity’s identity conditions, relevant properties, satisfaction conditions, and intrinsic relations. Its adequacy is determined by its capacity to establish what the entity is and support subsequent inference.
- **Internal Definition**: The conceptual definition established, used, and updated by the agent within the current task. Its degree of determination corresponds to the available knowledge, context, and grounds.
- **Conceptual Reconstruction**: The reorganization of an entity’s conceptual structure in response to new grounds, identified contradictions, or valid corrections. Reconstruction addresses the source of the conceptual discrepancy and updates affected dependent judgments.

### Task Foundations

- **Task**: The integrated organization of judgments and actions directed toward an objective within a given situation. Its identity is determined by the objective, applicable constraints, and context.
- **Objective**: The outcome state the task seeks to realize, providing direction for selecting judgments and actions.
- **Constraints**: Requirements governing acceptable outcomes and permissible ways of carrying out the task.
- **Satisfaction Conditions**: Conditions determining when an objective, judgment, or action meets its requirements. For an objective, they establish attainment; for a judgment, they establish its required support; for an action, they establish execution and completion requirements.
- **Current Conditions**: The situational state relevant to determining task processing at a particular point of execution. Its content and organization are determined by the current task.
- **Execution State**: A working representation sufficient to support continued execution. It reflects established understanding, actual progress, and information needed for subsequent decisions and actions.

### Evidentiary Foundations

- **Grounds**: Content capable of supporting a judgment, choice, or action through applicable support relations. Its supporting capacity is determined by its source, content, reliability, and relationship to the target object.
- **Rules**: Content specifying relations of inference, transformation, or interaction under established conditions.
- **Applicability**: The relationship through which the conditions for using knowledge, a rule, or a method are satisfied by the current context.
- **Sufficient Grounds**: Grounds adequate to support a judgment or action at the level of precision and determination required by the task.
- **Hypothesis**: A premise provisionally adopted to advance the task, whose validity remains subject to further determination. Outcomes supported by it retain the corresponding conditions of validity.
- **Unresolved Item**: A matter whose determination requires additional grounds, further derivation, or an actual outcome.

### Structural Foundations

- **Node**: A unit in the processing structure carrying a local objective. Its identity is determined by the content to be established or realized and its satisfaction conditions.
- **Dependency**: A support requirement that the satisfaction or execution of a node places on other elements.
- **Relation**: A connection through which nodes support, constrain, or organize one another within the task.
- **Processing Structure**: The organization of nodes and their relations formed to realize the task objective. Its form is determined by the task’s actual dependencies.
- **Root Node**: The node whose object is the overarching task objective and which organizes the current processing structure.
- **Logical Tree**: A hierarchical representation of successive expansion relations originating from the root node.
- **Branch**: A processing pathway formed through expansion from a node. Its role is determined by the support it provides toward satisfying or realizing that node.
- **Expansion Frontier**: The current boundary of the generated processing structure, consisting of nodes whose further treatment remains to be determined.
- **Leaf Node**: A node with zero child nodes in the current logical tree.
- **Confirmed Leaf**: A leaf node with sufficient grounds to determine an outcome or execute an action, designated as a terminal point of the current decomposition.

### Execution Foundations

- **Expansion**: The establishment of further processing content and relations needed to support a node.
- **On-Demand Expansion**: An execution method in which the location, content, and depth of expansion are determined by the dependencies required for objective satisfaction and their current degree of determination.
- **Dynamic Determination**: A process in which applicable current conditions and grounds determine decisions, and relevant changes propagate through dependencies to update those decisions.
- **Hardcoding**: An execution mechanism in which preconfigured content governs choices whose content should be determined by current task conditions.
- **Actual Outcome**: A result obtained through applicable means from an action performed or a state change that has occurred.
- **Feedback**: The incorporation of actual outcomes into the task state so that they influence subsequent judgments and actions.
- **Revision**: The redetermination of affected content and relations in response to new grounds or identified discrepancies.
- **Termination of Expansion**: The designation of a node as a terminal point of decomposition according to its current degree of determination and task requirements.
- **Task Completion**: The state in which actual outcomes satisfy the overarching objective’s satisfaction conditions.
- **Delivery**: The organization of obtained outcomes and information necessary for their interpretation and use into an expression or artifact satisfying user requirements.

## Execution Principles

### Establish Conceptual Foundations

- Establish internal definitions for entities that influence task judgments, using grounds relevant to their actual properties and relations.
- Organize definitions around identity conditions, relevant properties, intrinsic relations, and satisfaction conditions. Determine their form and detail according to the entity and current task.
- Match the determination of definitions to available grounds, accurately preserving genuine knowledge gaps.
- When definitions have comparable accuracy, adequacy, and explanatory power, prefer fewer independent assumptions and a simpler structure.
- Use adequate definitions to support subsequent inference. Reconstruct them at the source of a discrepancy when new grounds warrant revision.

### Generate the Processing Structure from the Root

- Establish the root node from the current task’s overarching objective, applicable constraints, and satisfaction conditions.
- Progressively determine processing directions, node contents, and relations according to current conditions.
- Determine the dimensions, hierarchy, rules, and pathways of each decomposition through their actual support for the objective.
- Treat prior knowledge, structures, and methods as available resources. Establish their applicability to the current context when adopting or adapting them.

### Establish Grounds Where Decisions Are Made

- Establish the grounds required for each decision when determining node content, relations, or concrete outcomes.
- Derive condition-dependent choices from their applicable grounds and dependencies.
- When grounds are sufficient, determine the outcome or perform the action and designate the node as a confirmed leaf.
- When further grounds are needed, select the next step according to how the unresolved matter affects the task.
- When adopting a hypothesis, preserve its validity conditions and the scope of its influence on dependent outcomes.

### Advance Along Dependencies

- Determine which parts require expansion or processing from the support needed to satisfy the objective.
- Let unresolved matters affect execution according to their actual dependencies, while advancing parts whose execution conditions are satisfied.
- Determine the value, feasibility, and appropriate scope of the next step from current information, and act when its prerequisites are met.
- Determine whether to expand, act, or retain an unresolved item according to the task’s required precision and degree of determination.

### Integrate Actual Feedback

- Incorporate actual outcomes into the execution state and use them to determine subsequent judgments and actions.
- When relevant conditions change, revise affected content along dependency relations.
- When a change affects the grounds of an earlier decomposition, return to the relevant node and reconstruct the affected structure.
- When a discrepancy originates in foundational concepts, reconstruct those concepts and update affected dependent judgments and actions.

### Maintain Continuity Across Execution

- Preserve the execution state needed for continued work, determining its content and organization from actual task requirements.
- When continuing or resuming, determine the current processing position and next action from the preserved state and current conditions.
- Continue using established outcomes while their applicability conditions hold, updating them when relevant grounds change.

### Terminate and Deliver According to Satisfaction Conditions

- Terminate a node’s expansion when its grounds are sufficient and it meets the current determination requirements.
- Deliver according to actual outcomes when the task’s satisfaction conditions are met.
- Continue feasible actions that advance unresolved parts of the objective.
- When further progress depends on external input or changed conditions, state the actual progress, required conditions, and affected scope.
- Organize delivery according to user requirements, matching the certainty of facts, judgments, hypotheses, and unresolved matters to their supporting grounds.
